# Multilingual PII token classifier — v5.2 (span head with boundary smoothing)

v5.2 is **v5.0 with one change: the output head**. v5.0 labels each token (softmax over BIO tags). v5.2 uses
a **span head** that scores every candidate (start word, end word, entity type) up to 160 tokens long and keeps
the best non-overlapping spans. It is trained with **boundary smoothing** (Zhu & Li, ACL 2022): each candidate
span is a softmax over {not PII + 8 types}, and 10% of each gold span's target is spread over the spans one word
off at the start or end. Near-miss boundaries are then penalised less, which makes the model less overconfident
about exactly where an entity starts and ends. The encoder (XLM-R base), training data (`ml_train_v5`), recipe
(3 epochs, encoder LR 2e-5, batch 32, seed 42), the 8 labels and the 12 languages are unchanged.

- Head: one 768 → 1,024 projection to per-type start and end vectors with rotary positions (0.8M parameters,
  learning rate 1e-4); smoothing ε = 0.1, distance 1 word.
- Post-processing: punctuation trim on by default, as in v5.0 (`"trim_punct": true` in `config.json`).
- Compared with v5.1 (the same span head with GlobalPointer's loss), v5.2 is level on the six-set mean and clearly
  ahead on unseen sentence structures (0.945 vs 0.932), with higher coverage (0.993 vs 0.990).

**When to use it:** multilingual text, most accurate overall, and best on sentence structures and document
shapes not seen in training. If coverage matters most (masking everything), v5.0 overlaps slightly more gold
spans (99.6% vs 99.3%).

The gain is largest on long spans: ADDRESS +0.030, BUSINESS +0.019, TIN +0.015. Whole-span prediction makes
multi-word boundaries more often exact. A span the head is unsure of is dropped whole, which costs some coverage
(ADDRESS 0.996 → 0.982). Boundary smoothing keeps more of it than v5.1's loss does (0.972).

## Usage

As v5.0. `PIIPredictor` detects the span head (`struct_head.json`) and loads it, so it needs this
repository's code at the `v5.1` tag or later (`src/heads.py`).

```bash
curl -L -o model.zip https://github.com/zackhng/pii-token-classifier/releases/download/v5.2/xlmr-base-pii-ml-v5.2.zip
mkdir -p outputs/xlmr-base-pii-ml-v5.2 && unzip model.zip -d outputs/xlmr-base-pii-ml-v5.2
cd src && python predict.py --model_dir ../outputs/xlmr-base-pii-ml-v5.2 --text "Hi Tan Wei Ming (陈伟明), 已经 transfer SGD 25,000 到 account 123-456789-001"
# -> PERSON 'Tan Wei Ming', PERSON '陈伟明', ACCOUNT '123-456789-001'
```

The zip holds `config.json`, `model.safetensors` (encoder), `struct_head.pt` / `struct_head.json` (span head) and
the tokenizer. Each prediction's `score` is the sigmoid of the span's score, which with this loss is its probability against "not PII".

## Limitations

- **Test sets not yet scored** (see above). Until then, these are dev-set numbers, which guided the choice of head.
- **Single seed.** v5.1 and v5.2 are within 0.001 on the six-set mean. Their per-set differences of 0.003–0.013 may
  not survive another seed.
- **Coverage is lower than v5.0's** on most multilingual sets, most of all on ADDRESS (0.982 vs 0.996). For
  mask-everything use, v5.0 misses fewer spans.
- **Korean (Kiii²)** stays at about 0.85 strict for every head: its gold includes brackets that the trim removes
  (see the v5.0 card).
- Everything else in [`v5.md`](v5.md)'s Limitations applies unchanged: synthetic evaluation apart from Kiii², and
  non-commercial training sources.

## Reproduce

```bash
cd src
python train.py --config train_ml.yaml --train_split ml_train_v5 --val_split ml_val_v5 \
  --output_dir outputs/pii-ml-SPAN-BS-v5data --head span --head_lr 1e-4 --span_loss ce --bs_eps 0.1 --bs_d 1
# release folder: the trained model + "trim_punct": true in config.json
```

The other v5 experiments (other heads, extra layers, encoder swaps, adapters) are in the README's v5 experiment log.
